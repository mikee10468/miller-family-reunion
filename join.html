📲 Install the app on your device

Installing adds a Miller Family icon to your home screen or desktop. Choose your device:

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the family app link in Safari (this must be Safari — not Chrome — on iPhone/iPad).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an up arrow ⬆️, at the bottom of the screen (or top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. Find the new Miller Family icon on your home screen and tap to open it anytime.

Android phones & tablets

  1. Open the family app link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install / Add to confirm.
  5. The Miller Family icon appears in your app drawer / home screen.

If Chrome shows a pop-up banner that says “Install,” you can just tap that.

Windows PC & Mac

Using Chrome or Microsoft Edge:

  1. Open the family app link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for the Install icon — a small monitor/screen with a down-arrow ⤓. Click it.
  3. (No icon? Click the ⋮ menu in Chrome → Cast, save and share → Install page as app; in Edge click ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.)
  4. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu (Windows) or Launchpad/Dock (Mac).

Mac Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): open the link, then choose File → Add to Dock.

🌳 How to add or update the family tree

Everyone helps build the tree. Your additions are reviewed by a family elder before they go live, so don't worry about making a mistake.

  1. Open the app and tap 🌳 Tree (also on your account page after you sign in).
  2. The first time, the app asks you to find yourself on the tree — pick your name so your posts and additions are tied to you. (Don't see yourself? Add yourself using the steps below, or ask an elder.)
  3. Tap any person's card to open it. You'll see options to Add child, Add parent, Add a sibling, or Add spouse, plus Suggest an edit to fix a name, date, or detail.
  4. Fill in what you know (name, birth year, etc.) and submit. You'll see “submitted for review.”
  5. A family elder approves it, and then it appears on the tree for everyone. 🎉

You can also add photos, RSVP for the reunion, post family messages, and join the family chat — all from the buttons on your account page.
